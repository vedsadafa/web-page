AEMOND TARGAREYN

House of The Dragon

Targaryen. Prince Aemond Targaryen, also known as Aemond One-Eye and later as Aemond the Kinslayer, was a member of House Targaryen. He was the second son of King Viserys I Targaryen by his second wife, Queen Alicent Hightower, and the younger brother of King Aegon II Targaryen. Like his siblings, Aemond was a dragonrider, and rode the dragon Vhagar.
